# ECHO: Brand Direction & Guidelines

## 1. Brand Philosophy
Echo is not just an app; it is a premium utility designed for intentionality. It is private, local-first, and highly curated. The brand should always feel like an editorial publication mixed with high-end audio hardware.

## 2. The Mark: Tangent Resonance
Our logo consists of three perfectly tangent circles on the left edge. 
- **The Core:** Represents the speaker.
- **The First Ripple:** The immediate articulation.
- **The Outer Boundary:** The lasting echo of the idea.
*Usage Rule:* Never alter the tangent point. The mathematical harmony must remain pristine.

## 3. Typography
- **Typeface:** Inter.
- **Display Weights:** Light & Medium with tight tracking (-0.04em) for dramatic headings.
- **Micro-copy:** Monospace (uppercase, tracking +0.2em) for UI labels, simulating a precise instrument.

## 4. Color System
Monochrome dominance. Black (`#000000`) and White (`#FFFFFF`).
*Exceptions:* We use extremely subtle, heavily blurred radial gradients (`opacity: 20%`) to cast an "ambient mood" from the active deck's accent color.

## 5. Motion Design
- Interactions snap with precision (Spring physics: stiffness 500, damping 30).
- Backgrounds breathe (8-second subtle pulse animations).
- The brand should never feel rigid or jumpy.
